About the Studio

A local-only Titanic souvenir card workshop.

What this is

This is an interactive browser tool that generates a twelve-photo front viewcard and a vintage postcard-style back page. It was themed around James Cameron's Titanic (1997) and Edward John Smith's RMS Titanic — launched 31 May 1909 in Belfast, departed Southampton on her maiden voyage 10 April 1912, and sank in the North Atlantic in the early hours of 15 April 1912.

How it works

Drag & drop grid

Drop photos onto the 12-slot grid or click any placeholder. Filenames like jack, rose, or ismay are matched to the correct character slot automatically via preset logic. Reorder any photo by dragging its slot to a new position.

Customise text

Edit the title banner and quote field in the Settings panel. The In Memoriam footer renders always. Six card templates colour-grade the final output; pick one and switch between Front Viewcard, Back Postcard, or Combined Sheet mode before printing.

Print / export

Click Print / Export PDF and choose your printer through the browser's native dialog. Everything renders on the client via the HTML5 Canvas API — your images never leave the machine and no third-party service touches them.

Privacy note

All processing happens locally inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Images never leave the machine. No analytics tracker, no cookies beyond the single tvs-theme key that remembers whether you prefer Dark or Cream mode, and no account — sign up, email, or sign in required.
