How do I create a Titanic-themed souvenir viewcard?

Upload photos by dragging them onto the grid or clicking the upload zone. The studio matches filenames like jack, rose, or ismay to the correct slot automatically. You can reorder any photo by dragging its slot to a new position, then switch between front, back, and combined views before printing the full sheet as a single PDF page.

What is the difference between the front viewcard and the back postcard?

The front is a twelve-slot photo grid styled as a 1912 souvenir viewcard, featuring the title banner, the famous line "I'm flying," a Titanic portrait at the top, and an In Memoriam footer for the 1,500 souls lost on April 15, 1912. The back is a vintage postcard complete with handwritten message lines, address rules, a stamp box, and an authentic postmark field defaulting to "WHITE STAR SOUVENIR WORKS • 10 APR 1912 • SOUTHAMPTON".

How long does it take to make a viewcard?

Most users finish a complete front-and-back set in under two minutes using the Load 12 Cast Preset button, which places sample images for every character instantly. Custom uploads with drag-and-drop reordering typically take three to four minutes depending on how many photos you want to personalise.

Steps to print your Titanic viewcard

  1. Select all twelve character slots by dragging photos onto each placeholder.
  2. Reorder the grid by dragging any slot to a new position until the layout looks right.
  3. Customise the title and quote text in the Settings panel; select any card template and layout option to shape the final output.
  4. Switch to Combined Sheet mode so both the front and back appear on one printable page.
  5. Click Print / Export PDF, choose your printer, and select double-sided if you want a physical postcard.

Is there a cost to use this Titanic viewcard studio?

The studio is free and runs entirely inside your browser. No sign-up, no account, and no images are uploaded to a server. All rendering happens locally through the HTML5 canvas API, so your photos stay on your device from upload to download.
